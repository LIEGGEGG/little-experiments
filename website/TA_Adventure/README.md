# TA Adventure

> 把技术工具书变成一段可以探索、推进并完成的学习旅程。  
> Turning a technical reference book into a journey you can explore, progress through, and finish.

**在线体验 / Live:** https://lieggeg.github.io/little-experiments/TA_Adventure/

## 中文

**TA Adventure** 是一个面向 Technical Artist 的交互式学习实验，以 Unity 官方电子书 *Unity for Technical Artists: Key Toolsets and Workflows — 2021 LTS Edition* 为主要内容骨架。

它不是简单地把 PDF 搬到网页里，而是把工具书重新组织成可探索的学习路线：章节、知识节点、学习进度、延伸阅读，以及完成旅程后的结局彩蛋。

这个项目更适合作为 **TA 知识地图**，而不是零基础入门课程。真正的目标不是记住所有内容，而是在以后遇到问题时，知道有哪些工具、系统和方向值得继续寻找答案。

本项目为独立学习与交互设计实验，与 Unity Technologies 无官方隶属或合作关系。原书、图片、文档及第三方资源的版权归各自权利人所有。

## English

**TA Adventure** is an interactive learning experiment for Technical Artists, primarily structured around Unity's official e-book *Unity for Technical Artists: Key Toolsets and Workflows — 2021 LTS Edition*.

Rather than reproducing a PDF in the browser, it reorganizes the book into an explorable journey with chapters, knowledge nodes, progress, further reading, and a small ending surprise for completing the adventure.

It is best treated as a **map of the Technical Art landscape**, not a zero-to-one beginner course. The goal is not to memorize everything, but to know what tools and systems exist — and where to begin when a real problem appears.

TA Adventure is an independent educational and interactive experiment and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Unity Technologies. Original materials and third-party resources remain the property of their respective copyright holders.
